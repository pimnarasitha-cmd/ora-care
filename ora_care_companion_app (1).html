75% Okra Mucilage Bio-Scaffold

High-molecular weight polysaccharide hydrogel providing 4–6 hrs sustained mucoadhesion over lesion.

8% Spilanthol Extract

Spilanthes acmella alkylamides blocking mucosal Na+ channels for rapid <5 min local numbing and xerostomia stimulation.

15% Riceberry Anthocyanins

Cyanidin-3-Glucoside ROS scavenging fraction to stimulate oral fibroblast migration and tissue regeneration.

Physician Administration Instructions:

Apply 1 Standard Patch (12 mm) directly over the dried lesion 15 minutes before meals. The bio-adhesive gel will self-hydrate and protect during mastication. 100% edible and safe to swallow.
